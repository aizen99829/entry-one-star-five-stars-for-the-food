An entry for the make.fun bounty **One Star, Five Stars for the Food**:
one sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist
reviewing planet Earth — one star overall, five stars for the food.

## The entry

> One star for Earth — gravity is a scam, the weather lies, and the locals queue an hour for brunch; the tacos alone deserve five stars, so I'm coming back hungry.

The review works because the complaints are the ones a tourist would
actually file: gravity that won't let you float, weather that promised
sunshine and delivered rain, and a planet where people wait an hour in
line just to eat eggs at noon. Then the praise lands on one specific
dish — tacos — so the five stars feel earned instead of generic.

The closing turn is the joke that holds it together: the reviewer
hates the planet and is coming back anyway, because hunger beats
principle. One sentence, three beats — complaint, complaint, praise —
held together with an em-dash and a semicolon, so it reads like a real
review rather than a slogan.

## How it runs

`main.py` validates the sentence (exactly one sentence, at most 200
characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
